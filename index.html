03 — ResQ deep dive
RESQ / 01

Emergency routing, safer travel and traffic intelligence.

ResQ brings together three routing experiences — Normal, SafeRoute and Emergency Services — backed by a web control layer for emergency operations and traffic-aware simulation.

01

Emergency Services
Nearest-ambulance assignment, pickup routing, hospital routing and live dispatch state.

02

SafeRoute
Women-focused routing using road width, activity, streetlights, reports and time-of-day context.

03

Operations dashboard
CCTV upload workflow, YOLOv8 detection, signal-control simulation and route monitoring.

04 — MiniPlay deep dive
MINIPLAY / 02

A relational database turned into a complete music experience.

MiniPlay was built with PHP, MySQL and the XAMPP stack, connecting a structured relational model to the user-facing experience. The interface covers discovery, organization and playback without relying on a third-party streaming backend.

01

Authentication
Login and sign-up flow for individual users.

02

Discovery
Browse songs, albums, artists and genres through a database-backed library.

03

Personalization
Liked songs, search and playlists turn the library into a user-specific experience.
