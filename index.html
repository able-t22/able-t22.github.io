03 — ResQ deep dive
RESQ / 01

Emergency routing, safer travel & traffic intelligence.

ResQ is structured around three experiences: Normal routing, SafeRoute for safer travel, and Emergency Services for ambulance workflows. The project also includes an administrative dashboard for monitoring and intervention.

01

Emergency Services
Ambulance assignment, pickup routing, hospital routing, and emergency simulation.

02

SafeRoute
Routing informed by road conditions, businesses, lighting, reports, and time-of-day context.

03

Operations dashboard
CCTV uploads, YOLOv8 detection, signal-control simulation, and real-time monitoring.
